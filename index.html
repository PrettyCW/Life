Click a white square to turn it black and flip the four adjacent squares. Drag from black to pan, and fling to coast. Use the wheel to zoom. Choose Manual World or Auto World from the menu. Auto World flips a random white square every five seconds and can be paused; manual clicks work in both worlds. The worlds save independently. Menu also contains Home, Square, saving, import/export, and World Snapshot. R starts a new world with confirmation.
